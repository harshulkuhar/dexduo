Privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026

This policy describes DexDuo and this support website. Contact kuharharshul@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Camera and imported images

DexDuo requests camera permission only for its scanner. Captured or imported images are processed on your device for recognition. The app does not upload those images or automatically save camera captures to Photos. You can deny camera permission and use Import instead. Images used by the scanner are held in memory while needed; selecting an image in the system picker does not give DexDuo unrestricted access to your photo library.

Pokémon data and artwork

The app requests Pokémon data from PokéAPI and downloads artwork from the URLs supplied by that service, including GitHub-hosted artwork. Those requests can reveal your IP address and the requested Pokémon or artwork to the service and its hosting providers. They are separate from local image recognition. Previously retrieved data and artwork are cached on the device; uncached entries need an internet connection.

Local preferences and purchases

Preferences such as automatic narration, daily tip-prompt timing and whether a successful tip has been recorded are stored locally. There is no DexDuo account system or developer-operated synchronization service. Reinstalling the app can reset this local state.

Where optional tipping is available, Apple processes payment through the App Store. DexDuo uses StoreKit transaction information to verify completion and avoid asking a supporter again. The app does not receive or store your payment-card or bank-account details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Support and website hosting

If you email support, you voluntarily share your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. The developer uses them to respond and investigate your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information. Email is handled through Gmail; see Google’s privacy policy. You can request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement. This site adds no analytics, advertising, cookies, external fonts or contact forms.

Your controls

Change camera permission in iOS Settings. Local app data can be removed by deleting the app; cached files may also be cleared by iOS. Deleting DexDuo does not delete App Store purchase records held by Apple or emails you have sent. DexDuo contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised effective date.
