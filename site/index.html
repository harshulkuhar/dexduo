DexDuo

A red cover. A world of Pokémon inside.

DexDuo brings name search, robotic spoken entries, evolution families, type defenses and on-device visual recognition to iPhone. Ordinary iPhones use a landscape layout; iPhone Duo adds a cover and unfolding experience.

The app is preparing for release. This site provides its privacy information, terms and support contact.
