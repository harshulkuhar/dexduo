Support

Questions, bugs or feedback?

Email kuharharshul@gmail.com.

For a bug report, please include the app version, iOS version, device model and steps to reproduce it. Attach a screenshot only if you are comfortable sharing it, and remove personal information first.

Quick answers

  • Camera unavailable? Check DexDuo’s camera permission in iOS Settings, or use Import. Simulator does not provide the physical camera.
  • Wrong recognition? Try one Pokémon in a clear, well-lit image. Recognition is not guaranteed; uncertain matches require confirmation.
  • An entry will not load? Uncached data and artwork require internet access. Retry when connected.
  • Ordinary iPhone layout? DexDuo runs in landscape. Duo switches between its cover and interior as the device opens.
  • Purchase or refund question? Apple processes optional tips. Use Apple’s refund instructions for a refund request.
